Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Welcome to Wearwell Closet. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data and user-uploaded media. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, stored, and protected when you use our web application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with a personalized digital wardrobe experience, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Your email address, profile display name, date of birth, and gender preferences entered during account setup.
  • Uploaded Media: Product photos, clothing items, and outfit photos uploaded directly by you to your digital closet.
  • Technical Data: Firebase Authentication logs, browser local storage records, and essential cookies required for session persistence.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information strictly to operate and maintain the Wearwell Closet service:

  • To authenticate your user session securely across devices.
  • To display, organize, and categorize your clothing items and custom outfit plans.
  • To maintain account security and prevent unauthorized access.

3. Storage and Data Security

Your privacy and media security are our top priorities:

  • Private Cloud Storage: All uploaded photos are stored in private Firebase Storage buckets protected by strict backend security rules (`users/{userId}/...`). Only your authenticated account can access or view your uploaded images.
  • Data Isolation: Firebase Firestore rules enforce strict per-user document permissions (`request.auth.uid == userId`), ensuring zero access for unauthorized third parties.
  • Transmission Security: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS protocols.

4. Sharing of Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or uploaded photos to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers under any circumstances.

5. Your Data Rights & Deletion

You have full control over your data within Wearwell Closet:

  • You can view and modify your profile details at any time.
  • You can permanently delete any clothing item or outfit memory from your closet directly through the application UI.
  • To request complete account and data deletion, please contact us at support@wearwell.app.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at support@wearwell.app.
